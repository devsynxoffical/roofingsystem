Overview

This case study covers a campaign we ran for a roofing contractor, reported in February 2026. 1,480 leads at $4.77 per lead between October and February for one roofing client. Below you will find the full result, what drove it, how the numbers compare with our other roofing campaigns and what you can apply to your own roofing company.

The result

From October to February, we generated 1,480 leads for one of our roofing clients at $4.77 per lead, with a $26.77 CPM — in a market where most pay $150+.

That's not luck. That's a system: the creatives, ad copy and audiences, the KPIs we track, and clear rules for when to launch, when to kill ads and when to scale.

What drove it

  • Tested creatives, ad copy and audiences
  • Tracked KPIs with clear launch, kill and scale rules
  • Sustained over five months, not a one-week spike

The numbers in context

  • Cost per lead: $4.77 is $6.64 below the $11.41 median across the roofing campaigns in our cost per lead study. Ranked from cheapest to most expensive, it is #4 of the 15 roofing campaigns on this site that reported a cost per lead.

How to apply this to your roofing company

  1. 1Build audiences around intentInstead of one broad audience, split targeting by homeowner situation and intent, and let the creative speak to each group. We teach this in the free mastermind training.
  2. 2Test creative until click-through clears 2%Creative is the biggest lever on Meta. Launch several angles, keep the ones above a 2% click-through rate and replace the rest. Cheaper clicks lead to cheaper leads.
  3. 3Build a system you ownAds, landing pages, CRM, follow-up and qualification work best as one system. Here is how we install it: roofing lead generation.
