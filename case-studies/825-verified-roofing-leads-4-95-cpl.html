Overview

This case study covers a campaign we ran for a roofing contractor, reported in November 2025. 825 verified roofing leads at a $4.95 cost per lead between October 25 and November 27. Below you will find the full result, what drove it, how the numbers compare with our other roofing campaigns and what you can apply to your own roofing company.

The result

While most roofers complain about the market, this US roofing company went all in and let the numbers talk. No excuses, no slow season, no "Meta is too expensive", just a clean offer, dialed-in creatives and a willingness to move fast.

Campaigns ran from October 25 to November 27: 825 verified leads at a $4.95 cost per lead, a $24 CPM in one of the toughest industries, a $1.33 CPC and $4,083 total spend.

Most roofers wait for leads. The smart ones engineer demand, and the platform responds immediately.

What drove it

  • A clean, clear offer
  • Dialed-in creatives
  • Speed: launching and iterating fast

The numbers in context

  • Cost per lead: $4.95 is $6.46 below the $11.41 median across the roofing campaigns in our cost per lead study. Ranked from cheapest to most expensive, it is #5 of the 19 roofing campaigns on this site that reported a cost per lead.
  • Budget: $4,083 in ad spend produced 825 verified leads.
