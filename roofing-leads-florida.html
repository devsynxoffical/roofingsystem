The problem

Why Florida roofers waste time on bad leads

More leads don't help if your team spends the day calling wrong numbers.

  • Fake and wrong numbers

    Unverified form leads include typos, fake numbers and people who never meant to submit.

  • Shared with competitors

    Bought leads often go to several contractors. You are in a race to the bottom on price before you arrive.

  • Crowded storm seasons

    When storms hit, feeds fill with roofing ads. Without a clear offer and fast response, you blend in.

  • Leads that go cold

    A lead that waits hours for a call has usually already talked to someone else.

Why phone verification matters for Florida roofing leads

An OTP-verified lead has typed in a one-time code sent to their phone, so the number is real and reachable. In our 16-day Florida campaign across 4 locations, all 365 leads were verified, synced to the CRM and nurtured by SMS and email before routing to sales.

Verified leads can cost a little more per lead ($19.50 in that campaign) but waste far less of your team's time. Compare that with campaign costs across 15 campaigns in our cost per lead study.

Multi-location and multi-service campaigns

Florida companies often serve several cities or offer more than roofing. The 365-lead campaign ran across 4 locations at once. Another Florida company covering roofing, gutters and solar got 280 qualified leads in 15 days with a click-through rate that held at 2.46%.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Florida roofing leads exclusive?

Yes. Ads run under your company name, so homeowners respond to you directly. Leads are never shared or resold.

What is an OTP-verified roofing lead?

A lead who confirmed their phone number by entering a one-time passcode sent by text. It means the number is real and reachable before your team calls.

Can you run campaigns for several Florida locations?

Yes. One of our Florida campaigns ran across 4 locations at once and produced 365 verified leads in 16 days.

How much do Florida roofing leads cost with your system?

In our Florida case studies: $3.75 per lead on day one of one campaign, $11.41 per lead for a 15-day campaign ($3,196 for 280 leads), and $19.50 per lead for a fully OTP-verified multi-location campaign. Costs vary by market and offer.
