The problem

Why Google Ads get expensive for roofers

Roofing is one of the most competitive categories on Google.

  • A crowded category

    Many companies bid on the same small set of searches.

  • The same few searches

    Searches such as "roof repair near me" and "roof replacement" carry most of the demand.

  • High cost per click

    With that much competition, the cost per click is high.

  • Budgets disappear

    Without tight targeting, negative keywords and a strong landing page, budgets disappear quickly.

Campaign types

The Google Ads options for roofing companies

A short guide to what each campaign type does.

  • Search adsShow up when homeowners search for roofers.
  • Local Services AdsPay per lead with the Google Guaranteed badge.
  • Performance MaxAutomated campaigns across Google, best with good conversion tracking.
  • Display and YouTubeRetarget past visitors and build awareness.

Google Ads vs Meta ads for roofers

Google wins for urgent repairs, because the homeowner is already searching. Meta wins for replacement and storm campaigns, because you reach homeowners before they search. Many roofers run both, with Google for high-intent searches and Meta for steady volume. See how we run Facebook ads for roofers.

Google Ads

  • Reaches people already searching
  • High intent, high cost per click
  • Limited by search volume
  • Bids rise in storm season

Meta ads (our system)

  • Reaches homeowners before they search
  • Our roofing campaigns: $3.75–$24 per lead, median $11.41
  • Scales beyond search demand
  • Creative and offer drive the cost

How to run Google Ads for roofing

1. Set up conversion tracking first.

2. Use exact and phrase match on high-intent searches.

3. Add negative keywords such as "jobs" and "DIY".

4. Target only your service area.

5. Send traffic to a landing page that matches the ad.

6. Call every lead within five minutes.

How much do Google Ads cost for roofers?

Cost depends on your city, season and competition. Clicks for roofing searches are among the more expensive in home services, and a lead often takes several clicks. Track cost per booked inspection, not cost per click, to see if the campaign really pays.

Semrush data from November 2026 puts the average cost per click for "roofing leads" at $22.36 and for "google ads for roofers" at $18.77. Judge Google next to every other channel in your roofing marketing on cost per signed job.

Should roofers use Google Ads or Facebook ads?

Use Google when you need people who are searching right now and you can afford high click costs. Use Meta when you want more volume at a lower cost per lead, with the offer and creative doing the work. Our system is built on Meta: see roofing Facebook ads and Instagram ads for roofers.

Do Google Ads work for roofers?
Yes, especially for urgent repair and replacement searches. They work best with tight targeting, conversion tracking, a dedicated landing page and fast follow-up.
How much should a roofer spend on Google Ads?
Start with a budget large enough to get meaningful data in your area for at least a month, then scale campaigns that produce booked inspections at a profitable cost.
What are Google Local Services Ads for roofers?
They are pay-per-lead ads at the top of Google that show the Google Guaranteed or Screened badge. You pay for leads instead of clicks, and reviews strongly affect your ranking.
Is Google Ads or Facebook better for roofing leads?
Google catches people already searching, while Facebook reaches homeowners earlier and often at a lower cost per lead. Many roofers get the best results using both.
How do I lower my roofing cost per click?
Improve Quality Score with relevant ads and landing pages, use negative keywords, narrow the location and schedule ads for hours when you can answer.
Why are my roofing Google Ads not getting leads?
Common causes are missing conversion tracking, broad keywords, slow or generic landing pages and slow follow-up.
