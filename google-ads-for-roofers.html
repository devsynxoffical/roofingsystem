The problem

Why Google Ads get expensive for roofers

Google works, but it comes with trade-offs worth knowing before you spend.

  • High cost per click

    Advertisers pay around $18–$22 per click for roofing lead keywords (Semrush, November 2026), before a single form is filled in.

  • Everyone bids on the same words

    Every roofer, lead seller and directory bids on "roofer near me". You compete on budget.

  • Storm-season spikes

    After storms, bids rise fast as everyone increases budgets at once.

  • Search demand is capped

    You can only buy the searches that happen. When they're gone, more budget doesn't buy more leads.

Campaign types

The Google Ads options for roofing companies

A short guide to what each campaign type does.

  • Search adsText ads on Google results for keywords like "roof replacement". High intent, highest cost per click.
  • Local Services AdsGoogle-screened listings at the very top of local results, charged per lead rather than per click.
  • Performance MaxGoogle's automated campaigns across Search, YouTube, Maps and Display. Easy to start, harder to control.
  • YouTube and DisplayVideo and banner ads that build awareness and retarget site visitors, closer to how Meta ads work.

How much do Google Ads cost for roofers?

Semrush data from November 2026 puts the average cost per click for "roofing leads" at $22.36 and for "google ads for roofers" at $18.77. That is the price of one visit; your cost per lead depends on how many visitors become leads.

For comparison, across 15 of our Meta campaigns for roofers the median cost per lead was $11.41, with the lowest at $3.75. See the full cost per lead study.

Should roofers use Google Ads or Facebook ads?

Use Google when you need people who are searching right now and you can afford high click costs. Use Meta when you want more volume at a lower cost per lead, with the offer and creative doing the work. Our system is built on Meta: see roofing Facebook ads and Instagram ads for roofers.

Frequently asked questions

Do Google Ads work for roofers?

Yes, they reach homeowners already searching for a roofer. The trade-off is cost: advertisers pay around $18–$22 per click for roofing lead keywords (Semrush, November 2026).

What are Google Local Services Ads?

Google-screened listings shown at the top of local results. Roofers pay per lead instead of per click, after passing Google's screening.

Is Google or Facebook cheaper for roofing leads?

In our experience Meta usually produces cheaper leads. Our 15 roofing Meta campaigns had a median cost per lead of $11.41. Google leads are often higher intent but cost more.

Do you manage Google Ads?

Our system is built on Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram). Tell us about your current Google campaigns on a strategy call and we'll show how the two can work together.
