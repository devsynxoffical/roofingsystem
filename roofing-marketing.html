The problem

Why roofing contractor marketing usually disappoints

Most roofing marketing fails for one of three reasons.

  • Clicks, not inspections

    The agency sells clicks instead of inspections.

  • Shared leads

    The leads are shared with other roofers.

  • Slow callbacks

    Nobody calls the lead back fast enough. A homeowner who asks for a quote and waits an hour has usually already booked someone else.

  • The fix

    Good roofing marketing fixes all three: exclusive demand, fast follow-up and reporting on booked jobs.

What roofing marketing should include in 2026

Homeowners now compare roofers on their phone in minutes. Your marketing needs short video ads that show real roofs and real crews, a Google Business Profile with fresh photos and reviews, financing options on the landing page, and instant replies by text. Roofing marketing ideas that work now

1. Run a free roof inspection offer for roofs older than 15 years.

2. Film a 30-second before and after video on every job.

3. Ask every happy customer for a Google review the same day.

4. Retarget website visitors with financing ads.

5. Send past estimates a seasonal follow-up text.

How to choose a roofing marketing agency

Ask five questions before you sign. Do I own the ad account? Are the leads exclusive? Can you show real roofing results with numbers? How fast are leads contacted? What do you report each week? How do you market a roofing company?
Combine a strong Google Business Profile, reviews, a fast website and paid ads to homeowners in your area. Then follow up with every lead within minutes and track which campaigns produce signed roofs, not just leads.
What does a roofing marketing agency do?
It plans and runs your ads, builds landing pages, creates video and image creatives, sets up follow-up and reports on results. The best agencies are measured on booked inspections and closed jobs.
How much does roofing marketing cost?
It depends on your area and goals. Most roofers budget for ad spend plus a management fee. The useful number is your cost per signed job, which should be a small share of your average roof value.
What are the best roofing marketing ideas?
Inspection offers, before and after videos, review campaigns, financing offers, retargeting ads and follow-up on old estimates are proven ideas that most roofers can launch in a week.
Is Facebook or Google better for roofing marketing?
Google catches people already searching for a roofer. Facebook reaches homeowners before they search, often at a lower cost per lead. Many roofers use both, with Facebook for volume and Google for urgent repair searches.
Which roofing marketing agency runs Facebook ads for roofers?
Roofing Systems Co. is a roofing marketing agency built around Facebook and Instagram ads for roofing companies doing $1M+ a year. It runs the ads, landing pages, phone verification and CRM follow-up, with published results such as 1,480 leads at $4.77 and $127,500 closed for a Dallas roofer.
How long does roofing marketing take to work?
Paid campaigns can bring leads in the first week. SEO and reviews take months but lower costs over time. A good plan uses ads for quick wins and organic channels for the long term.
