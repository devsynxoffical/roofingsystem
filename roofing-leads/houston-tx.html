The problem

What Houston roofers are up against

Houston is huge, spread out and hit by more than one kind of storm.

  • A metro too big to cover

    Leads from across the metro waste drive time. Campaigns need to follow your crews' areas.

  • Wind as well as hail

    Thunderstorm wind reports outnumber hail reports in Harris County, so damage offers must cover both.

  • Shared and resold leads

    Bought Houston leads often reach several contractors at once.

  • Long hurricane season

    The Atlantic season runs from 1 June to 30 November, and demand can spike at any point in it.

The Houston roofing market in numbers

Wind is the most common storm report. NOAA's Storm Events Database lists 110 thunderstorm wind reports in Harris County from 2021 to 2025, plus 77 hail reports (68 of them one inch or larger, the largest 2 inches) and 13 tornadoes (NOAA Storm Events Database).

Hail peaks in spring. Harris County hail reports were most frequent in March, June and May over those five years (NOAA Storm Events Database).

A very large, older housing stock. The city of Houston has about 1,023,112 housing units; about 48.3% were built before 1980, 42.0% of occupied homes are owner-occupied, and the median home value is $253,400 (US Census Bureau, ACS 2019 to 2023).

Storm seasons in Houston

Houston roofers deal with spring hail and wind, then the Atlantic hurricane season from 1 June to 30 November. A campaign built before the season can launch within hours of a storm in your areas. See storm damage leads and roof repair leads.

Roofing lead types for Houston roofers

Wind damage. Thunderstorm wind is the most common storm report in Harris County, so wind inspections lead after severe weather: storm damage leads. Repair. Storms and heavy rain bring leak and damage calls between major events: roof repair leads. Replacement. Nearly half the city's housing was built before 1980: roof replacement leads. Commercial. Warehouses, offices and apartment complexes across the metro: commercial roofing leads.

Areas near Houston we target

Campaigns can cover Houston and nearby cities such as Pasadena, Pearland, Sugar Land, Katy and The Woodlands, set by ZIP code. For North Texas, see roofing leads in Dallas; for the whole state, the Texas hub.
