The problem

Where roofing funnels leak

Most lost leads never make it past the page.

  • Ad and page don't match

    The ad promises one thing; the page talks about another. Visitors leave.

  • No trust signals

    No reviews, no real photos, no proof. Homeowners don't fill in the form.

  • Slow and long

    Slow pages and long forms on mobile kill conversions.

  • Leads go nowhere

    Form submissions land in an inbox instead of a CRM with instant follow-up.

Website contact page vs a dedicated funnel

Sending ad traffic to your homepage is the most common leak.

Homepage or contact page

  • Many links, no single action
  • Generic message for every visitor
  • Long contact form
  • Submissions in an inbox

Dedicated funnel

  • One offer, one action
  • Message matches the ad exactly
  • Short form with qualifying questions
  • Straight into your CRM with follow-up

Roofing website vs roofing landing page

A roofing website explains your whole company: services, service area, reviews and contact details. A roofing landing page has one job: turn visitors from one ad into qualified leads, with one offer and one form. Roofers need both, but paid traffic converts better on the landing page.

Why the page must match the ad

When the page repeats the ad's offer, proof and wording, visitors know they're in the right place. In our 215-lead campaign, the funnel and offer did the work: 2.33% CTR on the ad, validated leads at $12.79.

What happens after the form is just as important. See roofing CRM automation and the lead follow-up guide.

Frequently asked questions

Do you build roofing websites?

We build the pages that ads send traffic to: landing pages and funnels designed to convert and qualify homeowners. Your main roofing website stays; ad traffic goes to dedicated pages because they book more inspections.

Do you build the landing pages?

Yes. We design and build the pages and funnels, matched to each ad's offer and connected to your CRM.

Can I use my existing website?

Your website stays. Ad traffic goes to dedicated pages because they convert better than a homepage with many links.

Do the pages qualify leads?

Yes. Short qualifying questions and phone verification are built into the form.

How do you measure a landing page?

By conversion rate and cost per booked inspection, not just page views.
