The problem

Why roofing websites lose leads

Good-looking roofing websites still lose leads when these basics are missing.

  • No easy way to call

    Phone numbers that are hard to find or tap on a phone send homeowners back to Google.

  • Slow mobile pages

    Large images and heavy scripts make pages load slowly on the phones most homeowners use.

  • No proof near the form

    Reviews, licenses, warranties and real job photos sit on other pages, so the form feels like a risk.

  • Leads land in an inbox

    Form fills arrive by email, wait for hours and go cold before anyone calls back.

Brochure website vs a website built for leads

The difference shows up in how many visitors call or request a quote.

Brochure website

  • Many links, no single action
  • Generic stock photos
  • Long contact form
  • Leads sit in an inbox

Built for leads

  • One clear offer per page
  • Real jobs, reviews and licenses
  • Short form with qualifying questions
  • Instant CRM follow-up

What the best roofing websites do

They load fast on a phone, show the phone number at the top of every page, explain each service on its own page, show real job photos and reviews, and make the next step obvious. For paid traffic, they add roofing landing pages that match each ad exactly.

How much does a roofing website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, custom design, integrations such as a CRM and call tracking, and who writes the content. A template site costs less up front; a site built around conversion and SEO costs more and should be judged by how many inspections it books. We quote after reviewing your current site and goals.

Your website is where roofing SEO and paid ads both send people. A site with clear service pages, fast loading and strong trust signals lowers the cost of every lead from every channel. See the full approach in roofing marketing.

How much does a roofing website cost?

Do I need a new website or just landing pages?
Not always a new site. Many roofing companies keep their website and add landing pages for ads. We tell you which you need after reviewing your current site.
Will the website help my SEO?
Yes, when it has a page for each service and market, loads fast and is easy for Google to crawl. That is the foundation roofing SEO is built on.
Do leads from the website go into my CRM?
