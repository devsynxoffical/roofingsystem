Hail and wind damage roofing leads

Hail and wind leads come in short, intense waves. In Texas, spring hail often hits North and Central Texas from about March to June; along the Gulf Coast and in Florida, the Atlantic hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November. A storm campaign works best when it is built before the season and switched on within hours of a storm in your service area.

Target the ZIP codes and neighborhoods that were hit, not the whole metro, and send each area to a page that names it. One Florida client used hyper-local funnels for Miami, Tampa and Orlando with OTP verification, and the same structure works for storm-hit areas. See the seasonal detail for roofing leads in Texas and roofing leads in Florida.

How to get storm damage leads without looking like a storm chaser

After a storm, homeowners are wary of out-of-town crews knocking on doors. Ads that show your local address, your reviews and roofs you have finished nearby win their trust. Phone verification and an instant reply by text then show a real, organized company is on the other end.

Storm leads are also time-sensitive, so follow-up has to run around the clock: see roofing CRM automation. The ads themselves run on the system described in Facebook ads for roofers, and how much roofing leads cost explains what to budget for a storm push.

Storm and insurance roofing leads

Storm leads often involve insurance claims. Ads should explain how you help homeowners with inspections and claims, without promising claim outcomes. Keep messaging compliant with your state's rules on insurance-related roofing advertising.

We don't publish storm-specific case studies yet. The campaigns above show the parts of the system that matter most in a storm: fast launch, verification and follow-up. Read the roofing lead follow-up guide.

What are hail damage roofing leads?
Homeowners in hail-hit areas who ask for a roof inspection or repair after a storm. They are most valuable in the first days after the event, so the campaign has to be ready to launch and to respond within minutes.
When is storm season for roofers?
It depends on the region. Spring hail often hits Texas from about March to June, and the Atlantic hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November, affecting Florida and the Gulf Coast.
