The problem

Why storm leads slip away

Storm demand is real, short and crowded.

  • Everyone shows up at once

    Local roofers, out-of-state crews and lead sellers all target the same neighborhoods within days.

  • Slow launch

    A campaign that takes two weeks to launch misses the busiest days.

  • Junk and duplicate leads

    Storm leads are often resold to several contractors or filled in with fake numbers.

  • Trust

    Homeowners worry about storm chasers. Ads must show you're local and established.

Bought storm leads vs your own storm campaigns

Storm leads are worth most when they're yours alone.

Bought storm leads

  • Often sold to several contractors
  • Unverified numbers
  • No control over volume
  • Seller's brand, not yours

Your own storm campaigns

  • Exclusive to your company
  • Phone-verified
  • Scale up or pause in hours
  • Your reviews and brand in every ad

Storm and insurance roofing leads

Storm leads often involve insurance claims. Ads should explain how you help homeowners with inspections and claims, without promising claim outcomes. Keep messaging compliant with your state's rules on insurance-related roofing advertising.
