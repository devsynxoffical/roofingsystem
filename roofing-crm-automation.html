The problem

Where roofing leads go to die

The ad worked. The homeowner filled in the form. Then this happened.

  • Called back too late

    By the time someone calls, the homeowner has booked another roofer.

  • One attempt, then nothing

    Most leads need several touches. Without automation, follow-up stops after the first missed call.

  • Leads in five places

    Emails, spreadsheets, phones and inboxes. Nobody knows which leads were contacted.

  • No-shows

    Without reminders, booked inspections quietly don't happen.

Manual follow-up vs automated follow-up

Same leads, very different results.

Manual

  • Callbacks when someone has time
  • One or two attempts
  • Leads scattered across inboxes
  • No reminders, more no-shows

Automated

  • Text and email within minutes
  • Persistent follow-up until they book or opt out
  • Every lead in one pipeline
  • Reminders before every inspection

Frequently asked questions

What is the best CRM for roofing companies?

The best roofing CRM is the one your team actually uses, connected to your lead sources with automatic follow-up. Our case studies use GoHighLevel because it combines the pipeline, SMS, email, calendars and automations in one place.

Which CRM do you use?

Our case studies use GoHighLevel. If you already use another CRM, tell us on the call and we'll explain the options.

What is AI follow-up?

Automated follow-up that responds to leads and keeps the conversation going by text and email until they book an inspection or opt out.

Do I need your ads to use the CRM setup?

The CRM and follow-up work best as part of the full system, but they can be set up for leads from your existing sources too.

Will this replace my sales team?

No. It makes sure every lead is contacted fast and reminded, so your team spends its time on inspections and closing.
