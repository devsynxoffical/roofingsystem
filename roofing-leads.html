    Statewide lists send you homeowners your crews cannot reach.

    The same homeowner gets calls from every roofer in town.

  • Storm spikes, then nothing

    Leads arrive in waves after storms and dry up in quiet months.

  • Slow first contact

    Local homeowners book the first roofer who calls back.
