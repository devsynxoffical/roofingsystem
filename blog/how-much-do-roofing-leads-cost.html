How Much Do Roofing Leads Cost? 2026 Prices by Source

Roofing lead prices vary more by source than by market. Here is what each source costs, using our own campaign data, Semrush click prices and published vendor prices, with links so you can check every number.

By Roofing Systems Co. 3 min readUpdated November 23, 2026

Short answer

Roofing leads typically cost $4 to $25 each from your own Facebook and Instagram ads (median $11.41 across our 15 roofing campaigns), around $55 each from exclusive pay-per-lead vendors, and $18 to $42 per click on Google before a single form is filled. The number that matters most is cost per signed job.

Roofing lead cost by source

Facebook and Instagram ads

Across 15 of our roofing campaigns on Meta, cost per lead ranged from $3.75 to $24 with a median of $11.41. The lowest costs came with a specific offer, creative holding a click-through rate above 2%, and fast follow-up. Every campaign is linked in the roofing Facebook ads cost per lead study.

Google Ads

Semrush data from November 2026 puts the average cost per click at $22.36 for "roofing leads", $42.46 for "roofing leads texas" and $18.77 for "google ads for roofers". Only some clicks become leads, so cost per lead on Google is usually several times the click price. More in Google Ads for roofers.

Pay-per-lead vendors

Exclusive pay-per-lead vendors publish prices around $55 per lead (for example, 99 Calls Texas roofing leads). Shared leads are cheaper per lead but go to several contractors at once. See shared vs exclusive roofing leads.

Cost per signed job matters more

A cheap lead that never answers costs more than an expensive one that books. Track cost per inspection and per signed job. In our Dallas campaign, the result that mattered was $127,500 closed and 34 installs in about 60 days. Phone verification and fast follow-up are what turn cheap leads into jobs.

Lowering your cost per lead

Use a specific offer, real job creative, verified forms and instant follow-up. We build all four as one system: exclusive roofing leads.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Ask us on a free call or read the full FAQ.

How much does a roofing lead cost?+

From your own Facebook and Instagram ads, typically $4 to $25 (median $11.41 across our 15 roofing campaigns). Exclusive pay-per-lead vendors publish prices around $55. Google clicks alone cost $18 to $42.

Are cheaper roofing leads worse?+

Not necessarily. Cheap leads can be unqualified, but verification, qualifying questions and fast follow-up decide whether they turn into inspections.

Why are roofing leads more expensive in Texas?+

Competition. Semrush shows a $42.46 cost per click for "roofing leads texas", about double the national figure for "roofing leads". Our own Texas Meta campaigns still came in at $5.31 and $7.60 per lead.

Are roofing leads worth it?+

They are worth it when your cost per signed job stays well below the profit on a roof. Work it out as cost per lead divided by your close rate. For scale, a Dallas roofer in our case studies closed $127,500 from 34 installs, about $3,750 per job, so the cost to win each job has to stay well under that.

How much do shared vs exclusive roofing leads cost?+

Exclusive pay-per-lead vendors publish prices around $55 per lead. Shared leads cost less per lead but go to several roofers at once. Leads from your own Facebook and Instagram ads cost $3.75 to $24 in our 15 roofing campaigns, with a median of $11.41, and are exclusive to you.

What is the average cost of a roofing lead in 2026?+

Across 18 published roofing campaigns and 11,190 leads from Roofing Systems Co., the median cost per lead was $13.40, with a range of $3.75 to $24. Phone-verified campaigns had a median of $14.80 and standard campaigns $5.31. The full data is in the Roofing Lead Cost Report 2026.

What is a good cost per lead for roofing?+

One that produces booked inspections and signed jobs at a profit. As a benchmark, our 15 roofing Meta campaigns had a median of $11.41 per lead.
