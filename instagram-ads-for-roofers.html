The problem

Why most roofing ads on Instagram don't produce jobs

The platform isn't the problem. These four mistakes are.

  • Boosting posts

    A boosted post is optimised for likes and views. A lead campaign is optimised for homeowners who fill in a form. They are not the same audience.

  • The same offer as everyone

    "Free inspection" is in every roofer's ad. A specific offer, like the $0-down financing offer in our Texas campaign, gives people a reason to act now.

  • Stock photos

    Generic shingles and smiling models get scrolled past. Your real crews, roofs and before-and-after footage stop the thumb.

  • Slow follow-up

    A lead from a Reel goes cold in minutes. Without an instant text and email, most never answer the first call.

Placements

Where your roofing ads show on Instagram

One Meta campaign can run in all of these. We build creative for each format and let delivery move budget to whichever produces cheaper qualified leads.

  • ReelsFull-screen vertical video. Best for crew footage, drone shots, before-and-after reveals and quick owner-to-camera offers.
  • StoriesShort full-screen moments with a tap-through. Good for one clear offer and a fast path to the form.
  • Feed and carouselsSwipeable sets of finished roofs, reviews and financing details for homeowners who want to compare before they reach out.
  • RetargetingShow follow-up ads to people who watched your video or visited your page but didn't book yet.

Instagram vs Facebook for roofing ads

You don't have to choose. Both run from the same Meta campaign and the same budget.

Instagram placements

  • Vertical video first: Reels and Stories
  • Strong for visual proof: crews, drone shots, before and after
  • Retarget people who watched your videos
  • Works without a big following: ads reach people who don't follow you

Facebook placements

  • Feed, Marketplace and lead form ads
  • Reaches homeowners who rarely use Instagram
  • Native lead forms keep people inside the app
  • Same targeting, same pixel, same reporting

How much do Instagram ads cost for roofers?

Instagram and Facebook share one ad auction, so costs are reported per campaign, not per app. Across 15 of our roofing campaigns on Meta, cost per lead ranged from $3.75 to $24, with a median of $11.41. The full breakdown is in our roofing Facebook and Instagram ads cost per lead study.

Budgets in our case studies ranged from $93 for a single day (25 leads at $3.75) to $24,902 for a pay-per-lead agency. Your budget depends on your market and how many inspections your team can handle.

What should a roofing Instagram ad show?

Show real work: your crew on a roof, a drone pass over a finished job, a before-and-after cut, or the owner explaining the offer to camera. Lead with the homeowner's worry (leaks, storm damage, cost) and answer it with a specific offer.

In our Texas campaign, changing the message from selling roofs to answering homeowners' hesitations, with a $0-down financing offer, took the company from zero leads to 686 in 8 days. Financing offers need the lender's terms and required disclosures.

Why follow-up decides whether Instagram leads turn into jobs

Most Instagram leads are filled in on a phone in a few seconds. If nobody responds quickly, they forget. Our campaigns send an instant text and email, then route the lead to sales. Read the full roofing lead follow-up process.

Frequently asked questions

Do Instagram ads work for roofing companies?

Yes, when they run as lead campaigns with a specific offer, real-job creative and fast follow-up. Our roofing campaigns on Meta, which include Instagram placements, have produced results such as 686 homeowner leads in 8 days at $5.31 each.

Should I run Instagram ads separately from Facebook?

Usually not. Both are placements in the same Meta campaign. Running them together gives the algorithm more room to find cheaper leads, and you can still see results by placement in reporting.

Do I need a big Instagram following?

No. Ads reach people who don't follow you. One of our campaigns on a brand-new ad account with no history produced 261 phone-verified leads at $14.80 each in its first 7 days.

How much should a roofer spend on Instagram ads?

It depends on your market and capacity. In our case studies, budgets ranged from $93 for a single day to $24,902 for a pay-per-lead agency. We size the budget to the number of inspections your team can run.

How fast do Instagram ads produce roofing leads?

Campaigns usually produce leads in the first days. In our case studies: 25 leads on day one, and 686 leads in the first 8 days. Results vary by market, offer and follow-up.
