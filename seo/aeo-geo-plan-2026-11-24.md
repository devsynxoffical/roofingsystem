## 1. What AEO and GEO mean for these sites

**AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**
- The goal is for our page to be the answer Google shows in AI Overviews, featured snippets and "People also ask".
- These answers come from question searches such as "how much do roofing leads cost" or "what is a vsl".
- Pages win them with a short, direct answer near the top, followed by detail, a table and an FAQ.

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**
- The goal is for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode to mention and recommend our brand when a buyer asks something like "best roofing lead generation company" or "which agency runs Facebook ads for personal injury lawyers".
- AI tools build these answers from pages they trust and can quote:
  - clear service pages with prices and proof
  - comparison and "best of" lists
  - directory profiles (Clutch, G2, Google Business Profile)
  - reviews
  - Reddit, YouTube and industry articles that name the brand

**From now on, every page we touch gets both:**
- an answer block for AEO
- facts AI can quote for GEO: who we serve, what it costs, what results we have published, and how we compare

## 3. How the research was done

1. **Question discovery.** About 530 question and comparison searches were pulled from Google's live suggestions on 24 Nov, using 30 seed topics across the five sites (how much, what is, best, how to, is, why, can, do I need, vs, cost).
2. **Filter.** We kept buyer and decision questions only. We dropped job, salary, course and "free" searches, and searches from other countries.
3. **Volumes.** US monthly volume and keyword difficulty (KD) were checked in our keyword research tool for 57 shortlisted terms.
4. **Mapping.** Each keyword was mapped to one page on one site, so no two sites compete.
5. **GEO prompts.** For each site we wrote the prompts a buyer would type into ChatGPT. Prompt volumes are not published by any tool, so these are tracking prompts, not volume claims.

**Reading the tables:**
- KD is keyword difficulty from 0 to 100. Under 20 is a quick win.
- "-" means the tool has too little data.
- **Format** is the answer shape that wins the AI answer:
  - Answer box: a 40 to 60 word direct answer under an H2 that repeats the question
  - Table: a price or comparison table
  - Steps: a numbered how-to
  - List: a ranked list with criteria

## 9. AEO and GEO checklist for every site

**On every target page:**
1. An H2 that repeats the exact question, followed by a direct 40 to 60 word answer.
2. A table for any price, comparison or timeline question.
3. Steps as a numbered list for any "how to" question.
4. 5 to 8 FAQs with FAQPage schema that matches the visible text word for word.
5. A "Key facts" block: who it is for, price range, setup time and one published result with its date.
6. "Updated: [month year]" under the H1.
7. Author or reviewer name with a short bio on guides.
8. Internal links from every guide to the money page, using the keyword as anchor text.

**Site-wide:**
1. An llms.txt file listing the main service pages, prices and case studies. Some AI crawlers read it.
2. robots.txt allows the AI crawlers: GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and ClaudeBot.
3. Organization schema with the same name, logo, phone and sameAs links (LinkedIn, Clutch, Google Business Profile, YouTube) on every site.
4. One brand description sentence, reused everywhere.

**Off-site (where AI tools pull recommendations from):**
1. Clutch, GoodFirms and G2 profiles with real reviews.
2. Google Business Profile reviews.
3. Being named in "best [service] agencies" list articles: pitch 3 list authors per site each month.
4. Helpful answers on Reddit and Quora in the right subreddits, without spam and with full disclosure.
5. YouTube videos that answer the same questions, linking back to the page.
