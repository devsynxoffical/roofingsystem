## 4. Reddit answer drafts (r/RoofingSales, r/Roofing)

Rules: answer the question first, disclose who you are, link only when it adds data, no copy-paste across threads.

**Thread type: "Where do you guys get roofing leads?"**

> Full disclosure, I run a roofing lead gen company, so take this with that in mind. From our published campaigns: our own Facebook/Instagram campaigns ranged from $3.75 to $24 per lead across 18 campaigns (median about $13). The cheap ones were usually not phone-verified; verified ones cost more per lead but far fewer dead numbers. The bigger difference was speed: whoever texted and called within minutes booked the inspection. If you buy leads, ask in writing how many other roofers get the same lead. Happy to share the breakdown if anyone wants it.

**Thread type: "Are Angi/HomeAdvisor leads worth it?"**

> Disclosure: I work on the lead gen side. The per-lead price is only part of it. Ask how many contractors get each lead, what the membership or extra fees are, and what the refund terms are. Then compare cost per signed job, not cost per lead. For context, our own exclusive campaigns ranged from $3.75 to $24 per lead, but the number that matters is how many become inspections, and that comes down to how fast you call.

**Thread type: "How do I get leads in the slow season?"**

> Not a pitch, just what we have seen: some of our best campaigns ran October to February (1,865 leads at $5.24 from mid-October to mid-January). The ones that worked used replacement and financing offers instead of storm messaging, and followed up on old estimates. A Dallas roofer we worked with closed $75,600 in January alone.
