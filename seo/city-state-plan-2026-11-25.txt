 1. What the research shows
 We checked "roofing leads" plus 42 states and 30 cities in Semrush US.

  • States: only Texas (110 searches a month, KD 0) and Florida (90, KD 0) have demand. All other
    states get 0. That includes Georgia, Colorado, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Louisiana and
    Alabama.
  • Cities: 13 cities have small but real demand, all with KD 0 to 9. That means very low competition.
    One page per city can reach page 1 once the site is indexed.
  • Proof: the site already has local case studies, so its city pages can carry real proof that
    competitors cannot copy:
      ◦ Dallas roofer: $127,500 closed from 34 installs
      ◦ Florida: 295 verified leads across Miami, Tampa and Orlando
      ◦ Florida roofer: $111K in 31 days
      ◦ Florida: 365 OTP verified leads across 4 locations
      ◦ Texas roofer: $77K closed in 30 days
      ◦ Texas: 686 leads in 8 days
      ◦ Texas: 850 leads in 8 days at $7.60 per lead

 City pages here will not bring big traffic on their own. They do three useful things:

  1. They rank fast because KD is close to 0.
  2. They catch roofers searching "roofing leads near me" (210 a month, KD 14) from those cities.
  3. They give AI tools a clear answer to "best roofing leads in Texas" or "in Florida".

 3. Which pages to build

 Tier 1: weeks 4 to 6 (real local proof, or the highest volume)
  1. Upgrade /roofing-leads-texas into a real state hub. Add sections and links for Dallas, Houston
    and Austin, plus a "Texas roofing market" block.
  2. Upgrade /roofing-leads-florida into a real state hub. Add sections and links for Miami, Tampa and
    Orlando, plus a "roofing seo florida" section.
  3. /roofing-leads/dallas-tx: lead with the Dallas case study.

 4. URL structure
  • State hubs: keep the existing URLs /roofing-leads-texas and /roofing-leads-florida. They are
       already in the sitemap, so do not move them.
  • City pages: /roofing-leads/{city}-{st}, for example /roofing-leads/dallas-tx.
  • City hub: add /roofing-leads as a "Roofing leads by market" hub. It lists the states and cities, links
       to /roofing-lead-generation, and targets "roofing leads near me" (210, KD 14).

 Breadcrumbs: Home > Roofing Leads > Texas > Dallas. The state hub is the parent in the
 breadcrumb even though its URL is flat.

 Rules:

  • At least 800 words per page.
  • At least 60 percent unique compared to the other city pages.
  • No em dashes.
  • No fake reviews or offices.


 6. Internal links
  1. The homepage gets a "Markets we serve" strip linking to /roofing-leads, Texas and Florida.
  2. /roofing-lead-generation, /storm-damage-leads and /roof-replacement-leads each link to the 2
    state hubs.
  3. Each case study links to its city or state page. For example, the Dallas case study links to /roofing-
    leads/dallas-tx.
  4. Each city page links up to its state hub and across to 2 sibling cities.
  5. The footer lists Texas and Florida only.


 7. Sitemap and indexing
  • Add the city pages to /sitemap.xml, or to a separate /sitemap-locations.xml referenced from
    robots.txt.
  • After each city page goes live, request indexing in GSC URL Inspection the same day.
