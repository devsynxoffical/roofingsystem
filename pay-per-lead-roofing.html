Pay Per Lead Roofing: How It Works, What It Costs and the Alternative

Updated November 25, 2026

Pay per lead roofing means you pay a vendor a set price for each homeowner contact instead of paying for ads. It is simple and fast to start, but the price per lead is only part of the cost. Whether leads are shared, how they are verified and what you keep when you stop matter just as much.

The problem

What the price per lead does not tell you

Compare these before you sign with any pay per lead vendor.

  • Shared or exclusive

    A cheaper lead sent to several roofers can cost more per signed job than an exclusive one.

  • Extra fees

    Subscriptions, setup or membership fees on top of the per-lead price.

  • Refund terms

    What counts as a bad lead, and how fast you get a credit or replacement.

  • Nothing to keep

    When you stop paying, the flow stops and the pages and data stay with the vendor.

How does pay per lead roofing work?

A vendor generates homeowner requests through its own ads and websites, then sells each request to one or more roofing companies at a set price. You pay only for the leads you receive, which makes the cost easy to predict, but you do not control how the lead was generated or what the homeowner was promised.

Roofing lead prices

Prices vary by market, job type and exclusivity. 99 Calls lists exclusive roofing leads at under $50 per lead on its roofing page (99 Calls roofing leads). Google Local Services Ads also charge per lead rather than per click (WebFX on Local Services Ads). Across our own published roofing campaigns, cost per lead ranged from $3.75 to $24. Full detail is in how much roofing leads cost.
