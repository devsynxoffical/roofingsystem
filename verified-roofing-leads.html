What is an OTP-verified roofing lead?

OTP stands for one-time passcode. After filling in the form, the homeowner receives a code by text and types it in. If they can't, the lead never reaches you. It is the simplest way to guarantee the number is real and reachable.

In our Florida campaign across 4 locations, all 365 leads were OTP-verified at $19.50 each. A pay-per-lead agency received 1,024 verified leads at $24 each.

How to verify roofing leads before your team calls

1. Verify the phone number. The form sends a one-time passcode by text, and only homeowners who enter it become leads. That removes typos, fake numbers and accidental submits.

2. Ask qualifying questions. Short questions on homeownership, roof age, damage and timeline separate real buyers from renters and price shoppers, without making the form long.

3. Check it in the CRM. Every verified lead lands in your CRM, is checked for duplicates and gets an instant text and email, so your team calls a warm, confirmed homeowner. See roofing CRM automation.

This is how one campaign delivered 448 OTP-verified roofing leads at $20.10 in 14 days, and how hyper-local funnels for Miami, Tampa and Orlando produced 295 verified leads at $22.58.

Verified vs qualified vs exclusive roofing leads

Verified means the contact details are real: the phone number was confirmed with a one-time passcode. Qualified means the homeowner matches what you sell: they own the home, need roofing work and have a timeline. Exclusive means nobody else receives the lead. The best roofing leads are all three, which is what campaigns run under your own brand deliver.

Pay-per-lead agencies use the same verification for the homeowner leads they deliver to their clients: see lead generation for roofing agencies. The ads behind these campaigns are explained in Facebook ads for roofers.

Guaranteed roofing leads: what the guarantee really covers

"Guaranteed roofing leads" usually means the seller will credit or replace a lead with a wrong number or a duplicate. It doesn't guarantee the homeowner is ready to buy. Verification solves the same problem at the source: unverified numbers never reach you, so there is nothing to refund. Compare lead types in shared vs exclusive roofing leads.

Do verified leads cost more?

Sometimes, per lead. But verified campaigns in our case studies range from $4.95 to $24 per lead, and the real saving is in sales time. Compare costs across campaigns in our cost per lead study, and see why shared leads are rarely verified. For a full budget breakdown, see how much roofing leads cost.
