Short answer

Facebook and Instagram ads work for roofers when they are one part of a full system: a specific offer, a matching landing page, instant follow-up and lead qualification. Ads sent to a bare quote form tend to produce low-intent leads; ads backed by that system book inspections.

Why Facebook ads fail for most roofing companies

People scrolling Facebook or Instagram are not searching for a roofer at that moment. That is the core difference from Google search ads, and it is why sending cold social traffic straight to a generic "get a quote" form usually produces a pile of cheap but low-intent leads that never pick up the phone.

The ads themselves are rarely the only problem. In almost every account we review, the breakdown happens somewhere between the click and the inspection:

  • No real offer. "Free roof inspection" is what every competitor already says. Without a reason to act now, homeowners scroll on.
  • One vendor runs the ads, another built the page. Nobody owns the full journey, so the message on the ad and the message on the page do not match.
  • Slow or no follow-up. A lead that waits hours for a call has usually already spoken to someone else, or forgotten they filled in the form.
  • No qualification. Your sales team spends its week chasing renters, tire-kickers and jobs outside your service area.

This is what we mean on our homepage by roofing companies not having a lead problem but a client acquisition system problem.

What it looks like with the system in place

On a brand-new ad account with no pixel history, the same structure produced 261 OTP-verified roofing leads in 7 days at $14.80 each. For a Dallas roofer with strong reviews, it turned into $127,500 in closed deals and 34 installs.

What we build around your Meta ads

Ads are one layer. We install every layer as one system, so each part is designed for the others:

  1. 01Offer positioning. We work out an offer that is specific to your market and services, so the ad gives a homeowner a concrete reason to respond.
  2. 02Ad creatives and copy. Video, image and short-form creatives built for roofing, refreshed as performance data comes in.
  3. 03Audience setup and segmentation. Targeting built around intent, home value and service type rather than one broad audience. We walk through this in our free audience segmentation training.
  4. 04Landing page and funnel. A page that matches the ad's promise and pre-sells the inspection, instead of a bare quote form.
  5. 05CRM and AI follow-up. Every lead lands in a CRM and gets an immediate response, followed by email and SMS sequences so nobody goes cold.
  6. 06Lead qualification. Leads are filtered before they reach your team, so your crew spends its time on homeowners who are ready to move forward.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Ask us on a free call or read the full FAQ.

Do Facebook ads work for roofing companies?+

They can, when they are part of a full system. Facebook and Instagram reach homeowners who are not actively searching, so the ad needs a strong offer, a matching landing page, fast follow-up and qualification behind it. Ads sent to a bare quote form usually produce low-intent leads.

Are Facebook ads or Google ads better for roofers?+

They do different jobs. Google search ads catch homeowners already searching for a roofer; Meta ads create demand and stay in front of homeowners before they search. Many roofing companies use both. We specialize in the Meta side and the system that converts that traffic into appointments.

What does the system include besides the ads?+

Offer positioning, ad creatives and copy, audience setup, landing pages and the sales funnel, CRM setup, AI follow-up, email and SMS sequences, and lead qualification — installed and managed for you.

Who owns the ad assets and leads?+

You do. Landing pages, funnel, CRM, automations, creatives, copy, follow-up sequences and your customer data are 100% yours, permanently.

Is there a long-term contract?+

No. There is no long-term lock-in. Growth milestones for the first 90 days are agreed in writing, and if they are not hit we keep working with no management fee until they are.
